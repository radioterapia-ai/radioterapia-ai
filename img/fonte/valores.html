How we build, in six rules
01

Measure

before claiming. A verified number beats a comfortable estimate.

02

Review

every automatic output: it stays a draft until a physician approves it.

03

Protect

personal data and protected content. Neither circulates, whatever the intent.

04

Show

the error. A backstage with its failures beats a flawless showcase.

05

Teach

instead of delivering. Whoever learns comes back as a peer.

06

Serve

someone’s work before any audience. If it helps no one, it is not published.
